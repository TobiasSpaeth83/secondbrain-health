Privacy Policy

App: Secondbrain Health Sync · Operator and only user: Tobias Späth · Last updated: 25 September 2026

What data is accessed

With the user's consent, the app reads (read-only) sleep, activity/fitness and health metrics and measurements from the Google Health API. No data is written to Google.

How the data is used and stored

Data is stored only on the operator's own computer and in his private repository, and is used solely for his personal health journaling. It is not sold, shared, transferred to third parties or used for advertising, and it is not used to train AI models.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access and deletion

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Locally stored data can be deleted by the operator at any time.
