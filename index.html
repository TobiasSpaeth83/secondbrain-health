Secondbrain Health Sync

Secondbrain Health Sync is a private, single-user helper app operated by Tobias Späth for his own use. It reads only his own sleep, activity and health-metric data from the Google Health API and stores it locally on his personal computer, where it is compared with his own diary notes.

The app has no other users, is not offered to the public and does not share data with anyone.
